# Code of Conduct

The Luther Systems organization adopts the **Contributor Covenant**, version 2.1, as its code of conduct.

The full text is available at: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Scope

This code applies to all spaces managed by Luther Systems — including but not limited to GitHub repositories under this organization, issue and pull-request threads, project documentation, and any official communication channels.

## Reporting

Reports of unacceptable behavior may be sent to **infosec@luthersystems.com**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The privacy and safety of reporters will be respected.

## Enforcement

The Contributor Covenant outlines the enforcement guidelines we follow. Consequences are determined by Luther Systems administrators on a case-by-case basis, in accordance with those guidelines.
